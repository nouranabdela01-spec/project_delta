ASSET DIRECTORY: Recognition Videos
==================================
Project: Academic Achievements - Recognition Videos
Expected Files: MP4 video file (e.g. recognition-ceremony.mp4) or video thumbnail

How to activate:
1. Place genuine video or video poster into this directory.
2. In `index.html`, replace the placeholder in the Certificates & Recognition section with a responsive HTML5 <video> tag:
   <video controls poster="images/recognition-videos/poster.jpg" class="project-video">
     <source src="images/recognition-videos/recognition-ceremony.mp4" type="video/mp4">
     Your browser does not support HTML5 video.
   </video>
